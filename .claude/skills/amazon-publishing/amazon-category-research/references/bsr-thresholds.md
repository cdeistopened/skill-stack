# BSR (Best Sellers Rank) Thresholds

Understanding what BSR numbers mean for actual sales and category competitiveness.

---

## What BSR Indicates

BSR = Best Sellers Rank. **Lower number = more sales.**

A book ranked #1 sells the most in its category. A book ranked #500,000 sells very little.

---

## BSR to Daily Sales (Rough Estimates)

These are approximate conversions. Actual sales vary by category and time of year.

| BSR Range | Estimated Daily Sales | What It Means |
|-----------|----------------------|---------------|
| 1-100 | 100+ books/day | Bestseller territory |
| 100-1,000 | 25-100 books/day | Strong performer |
| 1,000-5,000 | 5-25 books/day | Solid mid-list |
| 5,000-10,000 | 2-5 books/day | Consistent sales |
| 10,000-50,000 | 1-2 books/day | Modest but steady |
| 50,000-100,000 | A few per week | Low volume |
| 100,000-500,000 | A few per month | Very low |
| 500,000+ | Rarely sells | Essentially dormant |

**Source:** [Kindlepreneur BSR Calculator](https://kindlepreneur.com/amazon-kdp-sales-rank-calculator/)

## Category Competitiveness Analysis

When researching a category, check the BSR of the **#1 book** and the **#20 book** in that category.

### Category Competition Levels

| #1 Book BSR | Competition Level | What It Means |
|-------------|------------------|---------------|
| < 500 | **Very High** | Major category, hard to crack |
| 500-5,000 | **High** | Competitive, needs strong launch |
| 5,000-10,000 | **Medium** | Achievable with good marketing |
| 10,000-50,000 | **Low** | Good opportunity, fewer sales needed |
| > 50,000 | **Very Low** | Easy to rank, but low traffic |

### The Gap Analysis

Compare BSR of #1 vs #20 in the category:

- **Small gap** (e.g., #1 = 5,000 and #20 = 8,000): Category has many strong books, harder to break in
- **Large gap** (e.g., #1 = 2,000 and #20 = 80,000): Top books dominate, but room to rank #2-10

---

## The Sweet Spot for Self-Publishers

**Target categories where:**
- #1 BSR is between 5,000-20,000 (category has buyers but isn't oversaturated)
- #20 BSR is under 100,000 (you can realistically reach top 20)
- The gap between #1 and #20 isn't too extreme

This indicates a category with:
- Enough demand (people buy books in this category)
- Achievable competition (you don't need massive sales to rank)
- Bestseller badge potential (realistic to hit #1 with a solid launch)

---

## Bestseller Badge Thresholds

To earn the orange "#1 Best Seller" badge, you need the **lowest BSR** of any book in that category at that moment.

In niche categories:
- You might need only **2-10 sales per day** to be #1
- During a launch, concentrated sales in 1-2 hours can spike you to #1 temporarily

In competitive categories:
- You might need **50-100+ sales per day** consistently
- Much harder to achieve and maintain

---

## BSR Updates

Amazon updates BSR approximately every hour based on recent sales. This means:
- BSR fluctuates throughout the day
- A burst of sales can temporarily spike your ranking
- Sustained sales matter more than one-day spikes for long-term visibility

---

## Quick Reference: Category Research Checklist

When evaluating a category:

1. [ ] Note BSR of #1 book
2. [ ] Note BSR of #20 book
3. [ ] Calculate the gap
4. [ ] Estimate daily sales needed for #1 (use Kindlepreneur calculator)
5. [ ] Assess: Can you realistically achieve this?
