# Category Selection Examples

Real cases demonstrating category strategy in action.

## Example 1: The Niche-Down Bestseller

**Author:** Dali Burgado
**Book:** "Defeat It!: A Woman's Guide to Crushing Life's Challenges And Finally Living The Fit Life"
**Genre:** Health & Fitness / Women's Health

**Strategy:**
- Instead of broad "Health & Fitness" category
- Drilled down to Women's Health subcategory
- Lower competition, targeted audience

**Result:** #1 Bestseller badge with minimal marketing spend

**Lesson:** Niche categories require fewer sales to rank #1. A bestseller badge in a small category is still a bestseller badge.

**Source:** [Kindlepreneur](https://kindlepreneur.com/how-to-choose-the-best-kindle-ebook-kdp-category/)

## Example 2: The Horse Training Discovery

**Book:** (Name not specified - Kindlepreneur case study)
**Genre:** Horses > Showing & Training

**Research Finding:**

## Example 3: The Description-Only Optimization

**Before:** BSR 540,557 (essentially invisible)
**After:** BSR 45,781 (11x improvement)

**What Changed:**
- Optimized category selection
- Improved book description with keywords
- NO changes to price or cover

**Lesson:** Category + metadata optimization alone can dramatically improve visibility. Many books fail not because of quality but because of discoverability.

**Source:** [AuthorImprints case study](https://www.authorimprints.com/optimized-book-description-amazon-search-engine/)

## Example 4: The 3-Category Portfolio

**Strategy (recommended by multiple experts):**

| Slot | Category Type | Target BSR (#1) | Purpose |
|------|---------------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | Super-niche | > 10,000 | Easy bestseller badge at launch |
| 2 | Mid-range | 5,000-10,000 | Solid ongoing visibility |
| 3 | Competitive | < 5,000 | Growth potential if book takes off |

**Example Application:**
- Slot 1: "Christian Living > Spiritual Warfare > Deliverance" (niche, easy badge)
- Slot 2: "Christian Living > Spiritual Growth" (mid-range, steady)
- Slot 3: "Religion & Spirituality" (broad, long-term play)

**Lesson:** Don't put all eggs in one basket. Balance quick wins with growth potential.

**Source:** [BookBloom category guide](https://www.bookbloom.io/blog/best-kdp-categories-2025)

## Example 5: The Launch-Day Badge

**Threshold for New Release Bestseller:**
- Depending on category, may require only **10-20 books sold in one hour**
- Concentrated launch efforts can spike ranking temporarily

**Strategy:**
- Select 1-2 achievable niche categories
- Coordinate launch day: email list, social media, ads
- Drive sales in short window
- Capture bestseller badge screenshot for marketing

**Result:** Author earns "bestseller" credibility even if rank drops later. Badge screenshot is permanent marketing asset.

**Lesson:** Bestseller badges are momentary but the social proof lasts. Time your launch strategically.

**Source:** [Book Launchers](https://booklaunchers.com/amazon-book-rank-and-sale/)

## Example 6: The Comp Title Research Method

**Process:**
1. Find 5-10 successful books similar to yours
2. Use BKLNK to extract their exact categories
3. Analyze: Which categories appear most often?
4. Check BSR in those categories
5. Select the best-performing shared categories

**Why This Works:**
- If competitors rank well in a category, the category has active buyers
- Their success proves the category isn't a ghost
- You're entering proven territory, not guessing

**Tool:** [BKLNK](https://bklnk.com) - Free, enter ASIN, see all categories

## Example 7: The Ghost Category Trap (What NOT To Do)

**Mistake:**
- Author selected 3 subcategories that looked different
- All 3 were ghost categories under the same parent
- Result: Book placed in only 1 actual category
- Lost 2/3 of potential category visibility

**What Should Have Happened:**
- Verify each category leads to a real Amazon page
- Ensure each selection provides unique placement
- Use Publisher Rocket or manual research to check for ghosts

**Lesson:** The KDP interface doesn't tell you if a category is a ghost. Always verify before publishing.

## Example 8: The Duplicate Category Advantage

**Fact:** 54% of KDP categories are duplicates

**Strategy:**
- If you're in a duplicate placement with good sales...
- Your book can appear in BOTH duplicate categories
- Effectively doubles your category presence

**How to Use:**
- Identify categories that are duplicates of each other
- Select one; Amazon may show you in both
- Not guaranteed, but possible bonus visibility

**Caution:** Don't rely on this. Prioritize unique, verified categories.

## Anti-Patterns: What Doesn't Work

### "Bestseller in Any Category" Gaming
- Selecting irrelevant categories just to rank #1
- Amazon penalizes this now
- Accounts have been terminated

### Broad Category Only
- Selecting just "Fiction" or "Non-Fiction"
- Drowns in millions of competitors
- No bestseller potential

### Set and Forget
- Categories that worked last year may be saturated now
- Quarterly review recommended
- Market conditions change

## Key Takeaways

1. **Niche beats broad** - 2 sales/day can make you #1 in the right subcategory
2. **Verify before selecting** - 27% of KDP categories are ghosts
3. **Use comp titles** - If similar books rank there, so can you
4. **Portfolio approach** - Balance easy wins with growth categories
5. **Launch timing matters** - Concentrated sales = temporary badge opportunity
6. **Optimization works** - 11x BSR improvement possible with metadata alone
