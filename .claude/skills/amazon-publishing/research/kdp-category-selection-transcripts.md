## Key Takeaways Summary

### The 3-Category Selection Strategy (June 2023 Changes)
1. **Old system:** 2 BISAC categories + could email KDP support for up to 10 categories
2. **New system:** 3 Amazon-native categories, no support requests allowed
3. You now pick from actual Amazon categories (14,000+) instead of outdated BISAC codes

### Ghost Categories (27% of KDP Categories)
- **Definition:** Categories that exist in KDP selector but don't have an actual category page on Amazon
- **Problem:** If you select a ghost category, your book won't appear in any browsable category - customers can't find it
- **Detection:** Only Publisher Rocket currently identifies ghost categories
- **Impact:** You waste one of your 3 precious category slots

### Duplicate Categories
- Many categories in KDP point to the same Amazon category page
- Selecting two duplicates = wasting a category slot
- Always verify category pages before finalizing selection

### Keywords for Category Placement
- Amazon uses your 7 keyword boxes to potentially add/remove categories
- Use category-specific keywords to "cement" your book in chosen categories
- Can potentially get placed in a 4th category by including those keywords

### BSR (Best Sellers Rank) Analysis
- Lower BSR = more sales
- Check #1 and #100 in your target category
- Gap between them indicates competition level
- #1 bestseller badge provides social proof and visibility boost

### Tools Mentioned
1. **Publisher Rocket** ($199-299 one-time) - Only tool that identifies ghost/duplicate categories
2. **Amazon Best Sellers pages** - Manual category research
3. **Kindlepreneur Sales Calculator** - Convert BSR to daily sales estimate
