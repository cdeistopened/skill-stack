# Content Idea: Homesteading for Real

**Status:** Idea
**Source:** Apple Notes brainstorm, Jan 2026

---

## The Concept

Videos that look like typical aspirational homestead content—beautiful shots, pastoral aesthetics—but with honest on-screen captions describing the actual reality.

## Examples

- *Beautiful sunrise over pasture* → Caption: "My wife got 8 stitches from the pig yesterday"
- *Peaceful animals grazing* → Caption: "The pigs won't stop fornicating"
- *Rustic barn scene* → Caption: "Lots and lots of poop"
- *Kids feeding chickens* → Caption: "This took 45 minutes of screaming to arrange"

## Why It Works

- Subverts the "homestead influencer" genre
- Builds trust through honesty
- Actually helpful for people considering this lifestyle
- Humorous without being cynical
- Still showcases the beauty, just adds reality

## Platform

- YouTube shorts / TikTok / Reels
- Could build real audience in homestead space
- Differentiated positioning vs polished competitors
