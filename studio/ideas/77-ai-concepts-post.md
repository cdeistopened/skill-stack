# Post Idea: 7 Concepts You Need to Understand to Succeed in 2026

**Status:** Idea for Skill Stack blog/lead magnet
**Source:** Apple Notes brainstorm, Dec 2025
**Note:** May have existing content somewhere to build on

---

## The Concept

Demystify AI jargon for normal people. Take concepts that sound intimidating and explain them in plain language.

## Example Concepts

- RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) → "AI that can look things up"
- Context window → "How much the AI can remember at once"
- Embeddings → "Turning words into math so computers can compare them"
- Fine-tuning → "Teaching the AI your specific preferences"
- Prompt engineering → "Getting better at asking questions"
- MCP (Model Context Protocol) → "Letting AI use other tools"
- Agents → "AI that can take multiple steps on its own"

## Format Options

1. **Blog post** - One big reference
2. **Lead magnet PDF** - Email gate it
3. **Twitter thread** - 77 tweets, one per concept
4. **Newsletter series** - 7 concepts per week for 11 weeks

## Why It Works

- People feel dumb about AI terminology
- Builds authority as "translator"
- Evergreen content
- Reference piece people bookmark/share

*The number 77 is arbitrary but memorable. Could be 50, 100, etc.*
