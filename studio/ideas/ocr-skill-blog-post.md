## The Thesis

Many will cling to old, less powerful UIs and pay handsomely for them. But there's a better tool now, native to CLI, that outperforms the commercial options.

This is a leading indicator of a bigger shift: **Anything you can't do via agentic tools in the command line is going to become a major hindrance** for a certain set of people.

We're moving toward a bifurcated world:
- People who learn CLI/agentic workflows → massive leverage
- People who stay in traditional UIs → increasingly limited
