# Brand Narrative Intersection

Using Alex Garcia's framework to define Skill Stack's content narrative.

Source: [Alex Garcia tweet](https://x.com/alexgarcia_atx/status/2012565627072819309)

---

## The Framework

Garcia's content narrative = the intersection of:
1. Your Mission
2. What You Believe
3. What People Will Rally Behind

Then distill to 3-5 words.

---

## Skill Stack's Intersection

### 1. Mission

Teach people to build lightweight content engines using AI-powered skills. Help creators turn their knowledge into shareable content through repeatable processes they own and understand.

### 2. What We Believe

- **Your raw material is enough.** You don't need more ideas - you need to transform what you already have.
- **Skills are personal, tactics are generic.** A skill shaped to your voice compounds; a tactic expires when the algorithm changes.
- **Get under the hood.** You don't need to be technical, but you need to understand the process. Graduated tinkering.
- **Transform, don't generate.** AI refines human ideas, not replaces them.
- **Own your workflow vs. the workflow owning you.** The enemy is the Borg - dehumanizing systems that treat people as cogs.

### 3. What People Will Rally Behind

- The possibility of "Wait, I could actually do this myself?"
- Turning knowledge into something shareable (the thing that feels impossible)
- A new creative middle class - solopreneurs and small teams competing with big budgets
- Content creation that doesn't consume your life
- Escape from the content treadmill and tactical churn

## The Distillation

### Primary (3 words)
**Skills that ship.**

### Supporting Copy
> Turn what you know into what you share.
>
> You have ideas. You have expertise. You have things worth saying.
>
> What you don't have is a way to get it out of your head and into the world - consistently, sustainably, without losing yourself to the content grind.
>
> Skills are repeatable processes that transform your raw material into shareable content. They're yours. You build them. You own them. And once you understand how one works, you can build more.
>
> This isn't about AI doing your thinking. It's about having a process you trust.

### The Feeling We Create
**Possibility** - "Wait, I could actually do this myself?"

### The First Win
A repeatable process they understand - they see how the skill works and could do it again.

### The Transformation
"I have knowledge but can't package it" → "I have a repeatable process I understand"

## Working Taglines (for testing)

Tested and discarded:
- Own your creative machine (too abstract)
- Exit the content treadmill (problem-focused, not solution)
- Transform, don't generate (existing pillar - good but not rallying cry)
- Work less, make more (close but felt like productivity hack)
- Skills are the new code (clever but cold)

Still viable:
- **Skills that ship** (locked as primary)
- Finish your work. Go outside. (lifestyle promise)
- Your ideas, repeated. (emphasizes the process)
