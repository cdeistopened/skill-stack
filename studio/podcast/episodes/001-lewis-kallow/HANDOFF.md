## Episode Summary

**Guest:** Lewis Kallow (ghostwriter, Every.to contributor, Action Digest co-writer with Scott Belsky)

**Core Thesis:** Examples beat instructions. The longer your AI prompts get, the worse your outputs become. Show the AI what you want through demonstration instead of describing it.

**The George Lucas Method:** When Lucas landed in Tunisia to shoot Star Wars, he didn't hand his crew a script and mood board. He showed them four films: Flash Gordon serials, John Ford westerns, WWII dogfight footage, Kurosawa samurai movies. Apply this to AI prompting.

## Key Concepts from Episode

### The Six Beats (Narrative Snippets Method)
1. **Setup** - The protagonist in their world
2. **Disaster** - Something disrupts the status quo
3. **Failed Approach** - They try the obvious solution, it backfires
4. **New Insight** - A realization that changes everything
5. **Resolution** - They apply the insight, it works
6. **Reflection** - The universal takeaway

### Via Negativa
Better results come from removing instructions than adding them. Keep templates tight. Trust the pattern.

### Skills vs Prompts
A prompt is a single-use instruction. A skill is a reusable pattern that improves each time you use it.
