# George Alexander: 0 to 1,000 Subscribers Framework

## The Core Insight

YouTube growth is **exponential**, not linear. You stay flat until ONE video breaks out.

---

## The Problem: Negative Social Proof

- Low views = viewers assume low quality
- Small creators trying to be "experts" lose to established creators every time
- The "guru" approach doesn't work when you have no audience

---

## The Solution: Lean Into Newcomer Status

**Relatability > Authority**

- People prefer following someone 1-2 steps ahead, not distant experts
- Low views on a journey video = "hidden gem" discovery feeling
- Vulnerability builds deeper connection than polish

---

## The Strategy: Document, Don't Create

Instead of "how-to" videos, document YOUR journey.

### Case Study:
- Generic growth tips → flopped
- "How Content Creation Completely Changed My Life in 90 Days" → broke out

### Success Example:
- Joe Faser's first video (body transformation) got 31M views
- People saw themselves in his STARTING POINT, not his expertise

---

## Implementation: Three Questions

1. **What journey am I currently on?**
2. **What are my genuine struggles?**
3. **What small milestone have I hit?**

Even small milestones (first 100 subs, finishing a 30-day challenge) are MASSIVE goals for someone else.

---

## Applied to Local Newsletter Video

### Journey:
Building a local newsletter using AI skills from scratch

### Struggles:
- Haven't done this before
- Figuring out the right stack
- Balancing with other work (Naval, OpenEd, family)
- Rural area = fewer sources than urban

### Milestones:
- Built Doodle Reader (the aggregation tool)
- Built newsletter-writing skills (from OpenEd work)
- Identified the market opportunity (SEO research)
- Have a thesis (AI does 80%, human adds 20% soul)

---

## Title Formula

**[Ambitious Goal] + [Starting Point/Vulnerability] + [Timeframe/Stakes]**

Examples:
- "I'm Building a Local Newsletter Empire with AI - Day 1"
- "Can AI Run a Local Newsletter? (Starting from Zero)"
- "I'm Trying to Make $5k/month from a Local Newsletter Using Only AI"
