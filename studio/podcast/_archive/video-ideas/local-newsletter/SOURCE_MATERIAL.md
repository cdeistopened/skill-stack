## Market Research (SEO Analysis)

### Competition Landscape:
- **Patch**: AI-driven newsletter for 30,000 communities, uses LLMs to aggregate local news
- **Bryan Collins**: Started leinsterinsider.ie, profitable local newsletter using AI + Ghost
- **Lookout Local**: Automated newsletters with 68% open rates using AI aggregation
- **NTM (Sweden)**: Scaled from 2 to 78 automated newsletter editions, 55% open rates

### Key Insights:
- Automated hyper-local newsletters = low maintenance + high engagement
- AI aggregates local data; human oversight ensures quality
- Open rates of 55-68% (vs ~20% industry average)
- Revenue model: Local sponsorships, subscriptions, event promotion

### Search Intent:
- "local newsletter business model" - LOW competition
- "AI newsletter automation" - MEDIUM competition  
- "how to start local newsletter" - MEDIUM competition
- "profitable newsletter ideas" - HIGH competition
