DeepSeek revealed that there are many innovations that can still happen at the level of training and prompting. there are still lots of “secrets” out there.

It used to be like an intern, now it’s actually about learning to manage someone who is in many ways smarter than you but isn’t going to bring their full intelligence to bear on a project unless you prompt them the right way.

1. What would so and so say about this?
2. Provide a framework
3. Narrow it’s field of vision.
4. Tell it to approach with blue sky thinking - don’t be afraid to be wrong.
5. tell them what you know, then ask, “what am I missing here?”
6. mix of experts
