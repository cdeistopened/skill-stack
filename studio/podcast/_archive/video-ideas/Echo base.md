# Echo Base: Intelligent Content System Architecture

## System Overview

Echo Base is your context-aware content assistant that amplifies your message across LinkedIn and X through intelligent content generation, curation, and strategic repurposing.

## Core Architecture

### Foundation Layer: Obsidian-Based Knowledge System

- **Context Documents** (stored as markdown on local drive):
    - What we do
    - Who we are
    - Vision/anti-vision
    - Topic tree + 1-line concepts
    - Core message framework
    - Content pillars and strategic narrative
    - Professional bio and ICP definitions
    - Swipe file of high-performing content formats/templates

### Three-Layer Content Processing Engine

### Layer 1: Substance Identification

- Analyzes incoming feeds (RSS + specific X accounts you follow)
- Cross-references with your topic trees and areas of activity
- Identifies alignment opportunities with your core themes

### Layer 2: Hot Take Development

- Multi-step reasoning process: "What do we really want to say about this?"
- Derives signal beneath the noise from popular posts and comments
- Generates substantive angles based on your context documents
- Applies SCAMPER methodology for content ideation

### Layer 3: Format Selection & Framework Application

- Intelligent routing to appropriate content templates from swipe file
- Matches substance to optimal format based on platform and engagement patterns
- Generates hundreds of possibilities before selecting 2-3 for testing

## Content Generation Types

### Original Posts

- Daily 5-10 content proposals rooted in your knowledge base
- Generated from combination of context documents
- Spans your topic buckets with voice consistency
- Jobs-to-be-Done theory integration

### Derivative Takes

- **Reposts with Captions**: Strategic commentary on curated content
- **Comments**: Thoughtful responses that reinforce your core message
- Real-time analysis of feed content for reaction opportunities

## Technical Implementation

### Data Sources & Integration

- **Local Storage**: Obsidian vault with markdown files
- **RSS Feeds**: Curated sources for content inspiration
- **X API Integration**: Monitor specific accounts and trends
- **Optional APIs**: LinkedIn, scheduling tools (future enhancement)

### Daily Workflow

1. **Morning Digest**: Email with content suggestions
2. **Review Interface**: Web-based editing platform showing:
    - Original tweet/content being referenced
    - Generated response options
    - Context reasoning and format selection
3. **Approval Process**: Edit, approve, and publish (manual initially)

### Content Categories by Platform

- **LinkedIn**: Professional insights, thought leadership, industry analysis
- **X**: Quick takes, thread starters, community engagement
- **Cross-Platform**: Repurposed content optimized per platform

## Advanced Features (Roadmap)

### Content Workshopping

- In-platform editing and refinement
- A/B testing for content variations
- Performance tracking and optimization

### Guided Content Development

- Topic tree expansion exercises

- **Top-of-Funnel Focus**: Brand and thought leadership positioning
- **Time Efficiency**: Maximizes output while minimizing founder time investment

- **Signal Over Noise**: Hot takes with substance
- **Cybernetic Feedback**: Evolving strategy based on performance data

    HOW TO WRITE AN ATOMIC ESSAY: A BEGINNER'S GUIDE
    
    Atomic Essay a 250-word, single-idea essay published in a visual screenshot. It was Dickie's solution to the need to write "something longer than a Twitter thread, but shorter than a blog post."
    
    STEP 1: PICK A SPECIFIC TOPIC YOU WANT TO TALK ABOUT.
    With so many (or little) things you can write about, you might suffer from analysis paralysis. The solution to this problem? Start by answering a question. By answering a simple question, your creativity muscle will immediately start to engage. Now, you have a starting point.
    
    Here are some writing prompts that will help your creative juices going today, no matter what industry you're in:
    
    - Write about what you're consuming.
    - Respond or expand on someone else's writing.
    - Teach a reader "How to" do something.
    - Share a powerful life lesson you learned.
    
    STEP 2: DECIDE WHO YOU'RE WRITING FOR.
    One of the first key lessons in Ship 30 for 30 is to know who you're writing for.
    We call these your "audience buckets":
    
    1. General Audience
    2. Niche Audience
    3. Industry Audience
    
    How to apply this:
    
    - Ask yourself, "who am I writing this topic for?"
    - Are you trying to reach a mass audience, or are you trying to reach a smaller audience?
    
    STEP 3: CRAFT AN INTRIGUING HEADLINE.
    If YouTube videos have a 5-second "hook," effective Atomic Essays have an intriguing headline.
    
    These are the five things that need to be in your headline:
    
    1. Who are you writing for?
    2. What are you writing about?
    3. How are you making the reader feel?
    4. What is the outcome you're giving to the reader?
    5. How much information can the reader expect from you?
    
    STEP 4: OUTLINE THE KEY POINTS OF THE CORE MESSAGE OF YOUR ESSAY.
    Your key points are the meat of your Atomic Essay. After reading your headline, readers will skim, not read, the key points of your essay.
    Only then will they decide to engage in your essay. So, you have another few seconds here to earn your readers' trust.
    
    How to apply this:
    01 Once you have a headline, come up with the points you want to talk about.
    02 Constantly ask yourself this question: "Are my key points relevant to my headline?"
    
    STEP 5: EXPAND ON YOUR MAIN POINTS.
    Your readers need to know what you're talking about.
    When you expand on your main points, you slowly build your credibility and authority. The core message of each main point can include:
    
    1. A personal story that gives the readers context of your essay
    2. Research to make your essay look more credible
    3. Background of where you got this idea from
    
    STEP 6: EDIT YOUR ESSAY TO APPEAL TO YOUR READERS.
    Writing and editing are two different tasks.
    
    - When you're writing, you are tapping into your creativity. Put all your ideas into a tangible piece of paper (digital or not) with no judgment.
    - When you edit intentionally, you're stepping into the reader's shoes.
    
    Remember, your essay isn't about you. It's about your readers. When you're editing, you've to look at your essay and have the courage to ask yourself, "Am I making this easy to read?"
    
    STEP 7: PUBLISH YOUR ESSAY ON ONE OR MULTIPLE PLATFORMS AND GATHER DATA
    The ship 30 for 30 writing challenge has one goal: to publish 30 atomic essays in 30 days.
    The more essays you publish, the more data that you can gather.
    We believe data helps you:
    
    - Understand what readers are enjoying from you
    - Decide whether to make more of that same content or try a different topic
    - Steer the direction of your online journey
