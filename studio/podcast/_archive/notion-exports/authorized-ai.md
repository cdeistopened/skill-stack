## [Authorized.ai](http://Authorized.ai) Business Concept Overview

A scalable AI-assisted publishing service that converts podcast content into books and mini-books, targeting the middle ground between self-publishing and expensive ghostwriting services like Scribe.

### Core Value Proposition

- **Lower barrier to entry:** Offering book creation services at $3,000-$5,000 instead of $40,000+ (Scribe's pricing)
- **AI-powered pipeline:** Automated transcription, content organization, and chapter assembly from podcast RSS feeds
- **Strategic niche focus:** Targeting "hungry niches" with staying power and underserved book markets

### Business Model

- **Dual approach:** Proactively identifying promising niches/podcasters OR accepting selective clients
- **Flexible pricing:**
    - Lower upfront fees for promising niches with royalty splits (e.g., 60/40, transitioning to 75/25)
    - Higher upfront fees for less promising niches with minimal royalty expectations
    - "Authorize" model where creators approve compilations of their existing content

### Technical Process

1. Transcribe podcast episodes with speaker diarization
2. Generate polished transcripts using AI with large output token limits
3. Create content databases from RSS feeds
4. Organize material via Obsidian for internal linking and tagging
5. Use AI agents to outline and assemble chapters
6. Add finishing elements: title, cover design, style definition

### Market Positioning

- **Inspiration:** "The Almanac of Naval Ravikant" by Eric Jorgensen (sold 1M+ copies)
- **Target niches:** Classical education, Ray Peat diet, Catholic Land Movement, peptides/gene therapy, etc.
- **Stand-alone brand:** Not personal branding, but a scalable publishing firm
- **Potential software product:** "Root Docs" - a tool to guide creators through the book creation process

### Go-to-Market Strategy

- Identify hungry niches using Amazon KDP analytics and category optimization tools
- Reach out to mid-tier podcasters (4,000-100,000 monthly listeners) with tailored pitches
- Present proof of concept: market research showing demand + gaps in current book offerings
- Attend podcast conferences with booth/demos to attract interested creators
- Leverage existing contact list of ~2,000 podcasters for outreach
