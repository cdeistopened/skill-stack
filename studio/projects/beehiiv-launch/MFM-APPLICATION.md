## The Pitch

There are millions of knowledge workers who stand to gain from Claude Code and the skills protocol right now. Most people who understand skills are technical. But the people who stand to benefit most are general knowledge workers - writers, marketers, consultants, educators, creators.

The AI newsletter wave happened. ChatGPT newsletters, The Rundown, Neuron Daily - they had their moment. But none of them are keeping up with the skills revolution happening among Claude Code users who are using it for "non-technical" purposes.

**What's at stake is nothing short of the biggest transformation of work since the Industrial Revolution.**

I'm not being hyperbolic. The same choice Pope Leo XIII addressed in 1891 - whether new technology concentrates wealth or distributes it - is playing out again. AI can make a few platform owners very rich while turning everyone else into gig workers. Or it can give solo operators the leverage that previously required entire teams.

I choose door two. And I've been building toward it since 2023.
