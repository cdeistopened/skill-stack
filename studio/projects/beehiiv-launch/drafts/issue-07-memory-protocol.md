# Issue 7: Memory Protocol

*Why your AI forgets everything.*

---

You spend an hour getting Claude dialed in.

You explain your voice. Your project. Your preferences. You show examples. You refine until the output is exactly what you want.

Then you close the tab.

Next day, you start fresh. Claude has no idea who you are. You explain everything again. The AI has amnesia.

---

This is the tax you pay for using stateless AI.

Every conversation is a cold start. Every session requires re-explaining. All that context you built? Gone.

Most people accept this as inevitable. It's not.

---

Here's the fix: two files.

**CLAUDE.md** - Your project context. Who you are, how you work, what matters. Loads automatically.

**NOW.md** - Your session state. What you're working on, what happened last time, what's next. Update at the end of each session.

That's it. Two markdown files. The AI reads them at the start. Your context persists.

---

What goes in CLAUDE.md?

Think of it as onboarding for your AI collaborator. Everything a new team member would need to know:

- What this project is (one clear sentence)
- Folder structure (where things live)
- Style rules (your voice, forbidden patterns)
- Key decisions already made
- Session protocol (what to read first, what to update)

Mine is about 200 lines. Took an hour to write. Saves me that hour every single day.

---

What goes in NOW.md?

The state of play. Updated at the end of each session:

- What you're working on right now
- What you accomplished last session
- What's in progress (checkboxes)
- Open questions (stuff that's unclear)
- What to do next (priority order)

Think of it as a handoff note to your future self. Read it, pick up where you left off.

---

Here's what changes when you use this:

**Session startup drops from 15 minutes to 2.** No more explaining yourself. No more "catch me up." Claude reads the files and knows the context.

**Quality improves.** The AI makes better decisions because it has better information. Your voice stays consistent across sessions because the rules are documented.

**Work compounds.** Every session, you update NOW.md. Over time, you build a log of decisions, progress, insights. Your project has memory.

---

The deeper insight: this isn't just for AI.

CLAUDE.md + NOW.md is version control for your thinking.

You're documenting your creative process. You're leaving breadcrumbs for future you. You're making your work legible to collaborators.

Even if you stopped using AI tomorrow, the protocol would be worth it.

---

Most people resist this.

"I don't want to write documentation."

"I already know how I work."

"This feels like overhead."

I get it. But the math doesn't lie. One hour of documentation saves hundreds of hours of re-explaining. The ROI is absurd.

And once the files exist, maintaining them takes 5 minutes per session. You're already doing the work. You're just capturing it.

CLAUDE.md + NOW.md is the foundation. Without it, every other skill operates in a vacuum.
