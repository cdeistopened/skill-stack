### Feed Store Ask

The feed store is ground zero. This is where you make or break early adoption.

**The pitch:**
> "I'm starting a free weekly email that digitizes the bulletin board — events, services, lost dogs, firewood deals. Saves people a trip. I'd like to put a small sign with a QR code near the board and at the register. In exchange, I'll list Bangor Feed as the 'Founding Partner' in every issue and link to you. No cost, just exposure."

**Specific asks:**
- [ ] Permission to place QR code flyer near bulletin board
- [ ] Permission to place QR code at register
- [x] "Founding Partner" status in exchange (free promo in every issue) @completed(2025-12-29T21:47:37-07:00)
- [ ] Optional: Hay price data for a future "Hay Ticker" feature

### Bodega / Grocery Store Ask

Same pitch, lighter touch.

- [ ] Permission to place one flyer in window or near register
- [ ] Offer to list them as a "Local Partner" if they want

### Post Office

Trickier — federal property. But worth a soft ask.

- [ ] Ask if community flyers are allowed on any board
- [ ] If not, don't push it. Focus on feed store.

## Phase 3: Permissions & Opt-Out Policy

### Do You Need Permission to List Businesses?

**Short answer: No.**

You're reporting publicly posted information from a bulletin board. This is journalism, not advertising. You're not claiming endorsement.

**Best practice:**
- If someone asks to be removed, remove them immediately. No friction.
- Add a line to the footer: *"Want to add or update a listing? Reply to this email."*

**Optional (not required):**
- When you call for a story angle, mention you're including them in the directory. "I'm also listing your info in the Bulletin — let me know if you'd like anything changed."

### Opt-Out Process

- [ ] Add footer line: *"To update or remove a listing, reply to this email."*
- [ ] Respond to any removal requests within 24 hours. No questions asked.

|--------|--------|--------|
