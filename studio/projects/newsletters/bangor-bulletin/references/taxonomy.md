# Content Taxonomy

## Directory Categories (Mutually Exclusive)

| Category | Contents |
|----------|----------|
| **Events & Gatherings** | Time-bound community happenings |
| **Goods & Provisions** | Things you buy and take home |
| **Property & Land** | Outdoor/land-scale work |
| **Home & Trade** | Structure/building work, skilled trades |
| **Animal & Livestock** | Vet, grooming, livestock services |
| **Family & Wellness** | Childcare, health, education, fitness |
| **Vehicles & Equipment** | Buying/selling/hauling vehicles, trailers |
| **Lost & Found** | Missing/found animals (special prominence) |
| **Local Landmarks** | Ongoing attractions, institutions |

## News-Worthy vs. Directory-Only

**News potential** (can become Lead or Quick Hit):
- Time-bound events with community stakes
- New services or offerings
- Missing/found animals (always urgent)
- Seasonal resources (firewood in winter)
- Anything with a story behind it

**Directory-only** (goes in The Board):
- Ongoing services without news hook
- Static listings (contractors, vendors)
- Recurring programs already established

## Listing Formats

### Directory Entry
```
**[Name]** — [Key details]. [Contact]
```

Example:
> **Firewood (Oak)** — Greg. $300, free delivery <20mi. (530) 370-4799

### Quick Hit
```
- **[Headline]** — [What, when, where]. [Why it matters]. [Contact]
```

Example:
> - **Roast Beef Dinner** — Friday Jan 3, 5-7pm at Bangor Hall. $15/$7. This is how the community keeps the lights on. RSVP: Edith (714) 316-9492
