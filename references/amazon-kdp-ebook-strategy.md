## The 90-Page Sweet Spot

**Why 80-150 pages works:**
- Perceived value: Justifies $19 pricing
- Quick to produce: 7 days max
- High completion rates → good for Amazon algorithm
- Easy to scale to paperback, hardcover, audiobook

## The Math

**Conservative model:**
- $19 price × 35% royalty = $6.65/book
- 14 sales/day = ~$2,945/month
- 442 books/month total

## Niche Selection Criteria

✅ Other books in topic make $400+/month
✅ Fewer than 100 reviews (low competition)
✅ Solves an urgent, painful problem

**Sweet spot:** Books with <50 reviews = less competition, faster ranking
