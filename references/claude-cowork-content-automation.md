## The Experiment

Automated content production with Claude Cowork + browser:

**Pipeline:**
1. Find a NeurIPS best paper (research)
2. Write a thread on it (writing)
3. Generate graphics on Krea (visuals)
4. Validate work with ChatGPT (QA)

**Result:** "Shockingly, it worked!"

## Implications

- Claude Cowork with browser access = full content pipeline
- Research → Write → Design → QA in one flow
- Could apply to:
  - Newsletter production
  - Social media content
  - Wiki article generation
  - SEO content at scale
