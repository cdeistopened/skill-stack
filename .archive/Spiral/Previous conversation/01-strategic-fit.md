## My Point of View on AI Writing Tools

### What's Broken

1. **Voice Erasure**: Most AI writing tools produce generic, homogenized output that sounds like "AI wrote this"
2. **Context Amnesia**: Tools don't remember your voice, preferences, or past work
3. **Reactive Only**: You have to ask for everything; the AI never anticipates
4. **Black Box Prompting**: Users can't see or customize how the AI behaves
5. **No Skill Ecosystem**: You're stuck with whatever the product ships

### What Could Be Transformative

1. **Voice as Infrastructure**: Writing style isn't a featureâ€”it's the foundation everything else builds on
2. **Proactive Collaboration**: AI that drafts, suggests, and configures before you ask
3. **Skills as Cartridges**: Modular capabilities users can mix, share, and remix
4. **Transparent Architecture**: Users can see and customize the prompts/workflows
5. **Social Layer**: Writers sharing styles, skills, and workflows with each other

### My Vision for Spiral 2.0

**The "Turn <this> into <that>" Engine:**
- Inputs: Podcast transcripts, YouTube videos, notes/braindumps
- Outputs: Polished drafts, social posts, emails, subject lines

**Workspace Design:**
- Left: spiral.md (highest-level config), Style/Identity/Memory, Content Library
- Middle: Chat/prompt interface for active collaboration
- Right: Beautiful markdown preview/output

**Key Innovations:**
- **Skills Architecture**: Move from workflows to swappable "cartridges"
- **Self-Destructing Wizards**: Onboarding that guides then disappears
- **Context Integration**: Notion, Apple Notes, seamless import/export
- **Social Layer**: Public library of styles and skills
