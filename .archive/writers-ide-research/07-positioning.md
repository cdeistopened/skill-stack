# Positioning: The Real-World Angle

> Skip the red ocean. Go where AI meets the physical world.

---

## The Competitive Landscape Problem

The AI content space is about to get hypercompetitive. Everyone will have access to the same models. The marginal cost of "content" approaches zero.

Two losing strategies:

1. **Compete on pure AI optimization** - Racing to the bottom against everyone else with Claude/GPT access
2. **Compete in the Content™ red ocean** - Fighting for attention on social platforms against infinite supply

The winning strategy: **Go where AI intersects the real world.**

---

## The Niche + Local Thesis

The most defensible content businesses will be:

1. **Niche** - Specific enough that general AI can't compete on depth
2. **Local** - Tied to physical places, relationships, or communities
3. **Real-world integrated** - Connected to services, products, or action

Examples that work:


The content engine amplifies what you do in the world. It doesn't replace having something to say.

## The Local Newsletter Case Study

*This is how I'm making it concrete for people.*

I'm building a local rural newsletter for my community. Why this matters:

### Why Local Beats Global

| Global Content | Local Content |
|----------------|---------------|
| Infinite competition | Geographic monopoly |
| Audience is everywhere | Audience is neighbors |
| No switching cost | Community switching cost |
| Ad revenue model | Direct service/sponsor model |
| Success = virality | Success = trust |

### What the Engine Does for Local

The Writer's IDE isn't about generating content from nothing. It's about:

1. **Systematizing local knowledge capture** - Meeting notes, council meetings, conversations become indexed source material
2. **Maintaining consistent voice** - Your personality, not generic AI voice
3. **Rapid repurposing** - One event becomes newsletter + social + archive
4. **Institutional memory** - The corpus remembers what happened three years ago

### The Business Model

```
Local Newsletter Revenue Stack:
├── Sponsored posts from local businesses ($100-500/post)
├── Event promotion partnerships
├── Classified/listing fees
├── Premium tier for deep archives
└── Consulting for other local publishers
```

None of this works without trust. The content engine protects trust through quality gates (Ralph loops) and voice consistency (skills).

## The DFY Service Model

Go-to-market: Done-For-You setup for people who would otherwise hire ghostwriters.

### The Pitch

*"You have things to say. You've been paying someone to help you say them. What if an AI could do it - but actually sound like you?"*

### What the Service Includes

1. **Source Audit**
   - Scrape all existing content (X, LinkedIn, blog, podcast)
   - Index in corpus
   - Identify voice patterns

2. **Voice Capture**
   - Run voice-matching wizard on best samples
   - Generate custom voice skill
   - Test against new content

3. **Skills Setup**
   - Configure 3-5 skills for their recurring content types
   - Newsletter format, social posts, whatever they publish regularly
   - Set up Ralph loops with appropriate judges

4. **Workspace Delivery**
   - Pre-configured Claude Code workspace
   - All skills installed
   - Corpus populated
   - Demo session showing how it works

5. **Training**
   - 2-3 hour walkthrough
   - Show them writing a piece start to finish
   - Document the workflow

### Pricing

**$90 lifetime license** for the software/skills package.

**$500-2000 DFY setup** depending on complexity (amount of content to scrape, number of content types, training time needed).

The lifetime license is the hook. The setup service is where the money is. Once they're set up, they don't need you - but they might refer others.

## Why Mac Desktop First

Not cloud. Not web. Local Mac app.

### The Reasoning

1. **Your files, your machine** - Writers are paranoid about their work. Local-first respects that.
2. **No subscription fatigue** - Lifetime license, not another $20/month
3. **Claude Code already runs locally** - We're wrapping existing infrastructure
4. **Privacy story** - "Your voice skill never leaves your machine"
5. **Offline capable** - Works on planes, cabins, wherever

## The Anti-Hype Positioning

What we don't say:
- "10x your content output"
- "AI does the writing for you"
- "Create content in minutes"

What we do say:
- "Your ideas, refined systematically"
- "Sound like yourself, but polished"
- "Transform the work you're already doing"

The difference: We assume you have something to say. We help you say it better and more efficiently. We don't promise to generate insight from nothing.
