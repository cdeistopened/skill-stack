# X Launch Playbook

Six composable skills for taking a product from zero to a viral launch on X (Twitter), plus a LinkedIn relay for delayed amplification. Genericized from a live tactical walkthrough so any founder can apply them to their own product and ICP.

**Source**: Tactical walkthrough by Cailyn Y. (@cailynyongyong), founder of Momo, delivered to guest founder Min (@minmax0501). X post: `https://x.com/cailynyongyong/status/2043832596304670749`. Raw transcript preserved at `TRANSCRIPT.md`.

**Track record cited in source**: Four product launch videos, each >1,000 likes and >100k views. Nine-month runway from 0 to a breakout tweet (+2,000 followers from a single post).

## Skills

| Skill | Use when |
|---|---|
| `x-account-warmup` | Account has <500-1,000 followers and you need engagement before a launch lands |
| `x-viral-template-miner` | You need post ideas that are already proven to travel in your niche |
| `x-launch-video-structure` | You're about to film/edit a demo video for launch day |
| `launch-video-hook-generator` | You need the 3-5 word phrase that lives in the first second of the video |
| `icp-roleplay-demo-script` | The demo needs a story, not a feature walkthrough |
| `x-linkedin-content-relay` | You have X content that worked and want to harvest it on LinkedIn 2 weeks later |

## Recommended order of operations

1. **Warm up** the account (`x-account-warmup`) until you hit ~500-1k followers.
2. **Mine templates** (`x-viral-template-miner`) from competitors shipping right now.
3. **Draft the demo**: hook (`launch-video-hook-generator`) → story (`icp-roleplay-demo-script`) → structure (`x-launch-video-structure`).
4. **Launch** on X.
5. **Relay** the winners to LinkedIn 2 weeks later (`x-linkedin-content-relay`).

## Notes on applicability

- Tech / developer audiences: X works because devs chase GitHub repos and open-source tools.
- Non-tech audiences (event marketers, ops, healthcare, etc.): LinkedIn is the primary channel; X is supplementary. The relay skill matters more than the warmup skill.
- These skills assume you are a founder or operator posting in your own voice. For agency repurposing, use `social-content-creation` instead.
